Daily Rehab

Thoracic Extensions

10 slow reps

AC joint unloading
Cues + why

Cues: Lie upper back on foam roller. Extend gently over it. Do not force the neck.

Why: Restores upper-back extension so your shoulder blade moves better and the AC joint is less compressed.

Thread the Needle

8 reps each side

Thoracic rotation
Cues + why

Cues: On all fours, slide one arm under the body and rotate. Move through the upper back, not the shoulder.

Why: Improves rotation for BJJ frames and reduces shoulder compensation.

Pec Minor Release

60 sec each side

Pec / chest stiffness
Cues + why

Cues: Use a ball or Theragun gently on the upper chest, just below collarbone and inside shoulder. Avoid sharp pain.

Why: Reduces forward shoulder position and upper trap guarding.

Band External Rotation

2 × 15 each side

Rotator cuff
Cues + why

Cues: Elbow tucked to ribs. Rotate hand outward slowly. Use a light band.

Why: Direct rehab for supraspinatus/biceps irritation and shoulder stability.

Wall Slides

2 × 10

Scapular control
Cues + why

Cues: Forearms on wall. Slide up slowly while keeping ribs down. Stop before pinching.

Why: Trains clean overhead movement without shrugging.

Face Pulls

2 × 15

Lower trap / rear delt
Cues + why

Cues: Pull band/cable toward face. Elbows high-ish, shoulder blades back and down.

Why: Balances BJJ’s rounded posture and reduces upper trap dominance.

Serratus Push-up Plus

2 × 12

Shoulder blade
Cues + why

Cues: In plank or against wall, keep elbows straight and push shoulder blades apart.

Why: Helps the scapula glide properly around the ribs.

Prone W to Y Raise

2 × 10

Lower trap
Cues + why

Cues: Lie face down on bench/floor. Make a W, then raise arms toward Y. Very light.

Why: Builds lower trap so upper trap stops doing everything.

AC Joint Isometric Press

5 × 10 sec

Pain calming
Cues + why

Cues: Palm into wall at chest height. Press gently at 30–50% effort. No pain spike.

Why: Useful for calming irritated AC joint and rebuilding tolerance.
